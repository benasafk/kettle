Kettle UML sequence diagrams

26 user journeys. Proposed behavior, with first-release, proposed and later scope labelled. Read time from top to bottom; solid arrows are requests, dashed arrows are responses. alt means alternative branches; opt means an optional path.

ChatGPT + panel includes the host bridge. Protected operations always enforce identity, ownership and applicable access. Durable jobs continue outside an open chat; read their status on return.
